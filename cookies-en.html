Cookies Policy

Information about cookies used on the Les Samaritains website.

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website. We may also use similar technologies (such as localStorage) to store technical preferences, including your cookie consent choice.

2. Controller and purposes

The data controller is Les Samaritains association. Cookies are used for website operation, security, preference storage, and, where applicable, third-party content interactions (e.g., social platforms).

3. Cookie categories

  • Essential/strictly necessary – required for core website functionality; these do not require prior consent under applicable law.
  • Preference – remember user choices (e.g., cookie banner preference).
  • Third-party – may be set when interacting with external services (e.g., social media platforms).

4. Legal basis (GDPR/ePrivacy)

For strictly necessary cookies, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and proper website operation. For non-essential cookies, the legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which can be withdrawn at any time.

5. Consent and preference evidence

On your first visit, the cookie banner allows you to accept or refuse non-essential cookies. Your choice is stored locally in your browser under the technical key lsm_cookie_consent with value accepted or rejected.

6. Storage duration

Cookie duration depends on category. Consent preference is stored locally until browser data is cleared or the user changes the choice.

7. How to manage cookies

You can manage, block, or delete cookies and local storage via your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may impact the availability of some website features.

8. Third-party transfers

When you interact with third-party services linked from this website (for example social platforms), those providers may process data according to their own policies. Please review their privacy notices.

9. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and consent withdrawal. To exercise your rights, contact assoslsm@gmail.com.

10. Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent EU supervisory authority or, in France, with CNIL.

11. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact us at assoslsm@gmail.com.
