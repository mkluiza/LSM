Cookies Policy

Information about cookies used on the Les Samaritains website.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you browse a website. They help pages function properly and improve user experience.

2. Types of cookies used

  • Essential cookies – required for technical website operation.
  • Preference cookies – remember settings such as your cookie consent choice.
  • Third-party cookies – may be set by external services (e.g., social media platforms).

3. Consent

On your first visit, a banner allows you to accept or refuse non-essential cookies. Your decision is stored locally in your browser.

4. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Please note that disabling some cookies may affect the functionality of certain pages.

5. Personal data

We do not sell personal data and do not use cookies for aggressive commercial profiling. Data submitted via forms is used only for communication.

6. Contact

For any question about this policy, contact us at assoslsm@gmail.com.
