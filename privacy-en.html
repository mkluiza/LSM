Privacy Policy

Detailed information on personal data processing under GDPR.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Les Samaritains association (French non-profit, loi 1901), operating in Meaux, France. For data-protection questions, contact us at assoslsm@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

We may process the following categories of data: name, email address, phone number, subject and message content submitted through contact forms, as well as technical data strictly necessary for website operation.

3. Purposes of processing

  • handling requests submitted through contact forms;
  • communicating with volunteers, donors, partners, or beneficiaries;
  • technical administration and website security;
  • compliance with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal bases (Art. 6 GDPR)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) – when you voluntarily submit data via forms;
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) – for communication management and security;
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) – where retention is legally required.

5. Recipients and processors

Data is accessible only to authorized association members and technical service providers involved in operating services (for example email delivery), strictly on a need-to-know basis and subject to appropriate contractual safeguards.

6. International transfers

If any technical service involves transfers outside the EEA, we implement appropriate safeguards (e.g., standard contractual clauses), in accordance with GDPR.

7. Data retention

Data is retained for the time necessary to process your request and then only as needed for legal compliance, defense of legal claims, or mandatory archiving requirements.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, contact us at assoslsm@gmail.com.

9. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent EU supervisory authority or, in France, with CNIL, if you believe data processing does not comply with applicable law.

10. Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or unlawful disclosure.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

For details on cookies, consent, and your options, please see our Cookies Policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect legal or operational changes. The version published on this page is the version applicable at the date of consultation.
