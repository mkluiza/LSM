Official launch of "Les Samaritains"

We are pleased to announce the launch of our association, dedicated to solidarity and mutual aid. After months of preparation, our team of passionate volunteers is finally ready to serve people and families in need.

Our mission is clear: to provide concrete help and moral support to those going through difficult times. Whether through material donations, attentive listening or personalized support, we are here to make a difference.

CSEN Proposal: Simplifying Past Participle Agreement

For some, past participle agreement is like coding without debugging: it crashes every time. The National Scientific Council of Education (CSEN) proposes to simplify past participle agreement to make it more accessible to students and the general public:

  • With "avoir": the past participle becomes invariable
  • With "être": systematic agreement with the subject

This reform aims to reduce complexity, save learning time and promote mastery of the French language. However, it sparks debate among linguists and teachers, some wishing to preserve the historical subtlety of grammar.

🎉 Calendar of Rights for Romanians in France

Today, our association launches the Calendar of Rights for Romanians in France 🇷🇴🇫🇷 Each day, we publish an essential right that every person of Romanian origin living in France should know: social rights, access to housing, work, health, and much more!

Our goal: to inform, empower and support the Romanian community in accessing their rights. Because knowing your rights means being able to claim them.

⏰️ History corner — Volunteering through the ages

Since antiquity, Greek and Roman citizens participated in collective public actions. The 1901 law in France gave a legal framework to voluntary commitment. Today, nearly 20 million French people volunteer — and Les Samaritains carry on this wonderful tradition. 💛
