Our Mission

Working for a fairer society

We create a bridge between those who can help and those in need.

Solidarity & Mutual Aid

For nearly a year, Les Samaritains have been committed daily to helping people and families in need. Our mission is to create a bridge between those who can help and those who need it.

We firmly believe that every gesture counts and that solidarity is the key to building a fairer and more equitable society for all.

Our Actions

Concrete initiatives

Meeting the essential needs of people in difficulty.

🍽️

Food Aid

Distribution of food packages to families in need. We organize food collections for people in difficulty.

👗

Clothing Aid

Collection and redistribution of clothes, shoes and accessories for all ages. Solidarity wardrobe open to all.

📚

School Support

Support with administrative and school procedures for children from disadvantaged families.

🩺

Health & Wellness

Support with medical procedures and awareness of preventive health.

🎭

Social Activities

Organization of cultural events, outings and activities to combat isolation and create social bonds.
